# Lean Canvas: SmartProductDiscoveryAISystem

> Business model hypothesis untuk AI-powered product discovery platform

---

## Section 1: Product Definition

### 1. Problem

| # | Problem | Existing Alternatives |
|---|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Product discovery manual, lambat, dan tidak terstruktur** — PMs tidak tahu framework mana yang digunakan kapan dan bagaimana | ChatGPT/Claude generik, template Notion/Miro, membaca buku PM |
| 2 | **42% startup gagal karena "no market need"** — Discovery sering di-skip atau dilakukan dengan buruk | Skip discovery dan langsung build, hire konsultan mahal ($5K-20K) |
| 3 | **Insight discovery tersebar dan hilang** — Tidak ada centralized knowledge base untuk product insights | Google Docs, Slack threads, Confluence pages yang tidak searchable |
| 4 | **Keputusan produk berbasis opini, bukan evidence** — HIPPO (Highest Paid Person's Opinion) culture | Meeting-based decisions, gut feeling, competitor copying |

### 2. Solution

| # | Feature | Menyelesaikan Problem |
|---|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | **AI Discovery Guide** — Step-by-step AI assistant yang memandu discovery menggunakan framework terbukti | #1: Struktur dan guidance |
| 2 | **Automated Insight Synthesis** — AI mensintesis riset (interview, survey, feedback) menjadi actionable insights | #1 & #2: Kecepatan dan kualitas |
| 3 | **Hypothesis & Experiment Engine** — Generate, track, dan validate discovery hypotheses dengan eksperimen yang direkomendasikan AI | #2: Validasi sebelum build |
| 4 | **Insight Repository** — Centralized, searchable knowledge base untuk semua discovery insights | #3: Knowledge persistence |
| 5 | **Discovery Dashboard** — Metrics dan visibility ke discovery activity dan outcomes | #4: Evidence-driven culture |

### 3. Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

**Headline UVP:**
> "Your AI-powered Product Discovery Copilot — From Idea to Validated Hypothesis in Hours, Not Weeks"

**Why different:**
- Bukan sekadar template — AI yang **berpikir** seperti PM senior
- Bukan sekadar chatbot — **Workflow discovery end-to-end** yang terstruktur
- Bukan sekadar tools — **Knowledge system** yang belajar dari setiap discovery cycle

**High-Level Concept:**
"It's like having a senior PM mentor + research analyst + framework expert, available 24/7, embedded in your workflow."

### 4. Unfair Advantage

| Advantage | Description | Difficulty to Copy |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Framework Encoding** | Deep encoding dari 20+ PM frameworks (bukan template, tapi reasoning) | High — membutuhkan PM expertise + AI engineering |
| **Discovery Data Flywheel** | Semakin banyak discovery → semakin baik AI recommendations | High — network effect dari data |
| **PM Domain Moat** | Team dengan deep PM expertise (bukan "AI wrapper" team) | Medium — bisa di-replikasi dengan hire yang tepat |
| **Community Trust** | Brand trust dari PM community engagement dan content | Medium — membutuhkan waktu |

## Section 2: Market & Traction

### 5. Customer Segments

| Segment | Description | Size Est. | Priority |
|---------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| **Startup PM / Founder** | Individual PMs dan founders yang perlu validate ideas | ~500K globally | P0 (Beachhead) |
| **Mid-size PM Teams** | Product teams di companies 50-500 employees | ~200K teams | P1 |
| **Enterprise Product Org** | VP Product / CPO di companies 500+ employees | ~50K orgs | P2 |

**Early Adopters:**
- PMs yang sudah menggunakan AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude) untuk discovery tapi merasa kurang terstruktur
- Startup founders yang baru saja mengikuti PM course (Product School, Reforge)
- PM communities members (Mind the Product, Product School, Lenny's Newsletter subscribers)

### 6. Channels

| Channel | Type | Cost | Expected CAC |
|---------|------|------|-------------|
| **Content Marketing** (Blog, Framework Guides, AI Discovery tutorials) | Inbound | Low | $20-50 |
| **PM Communities** (Slack groups, Reddit r/product, LinkedIn) | Community | Low | $10-30 |
| **Product Hunt Launch** | Launch | Low (time investment) | $5-15 |
| **Integration Marketplaces** (Jira, Notion, Slack) | PLG | Medium | $30-80 |
| **SEO (PM frameworks, discovery guides)** | Inbound | Medium upfront | $15-40 |
| **Conferences & Speaking** (Mind the Product, ProductCon) | Event | High | $100-300 |
| **Enterprise Sales** (Phase 2) | Outbound | High | $2K-5K |

### 7. Revenue Streams

| Tier | Price | Target | Features |
|------|-------|--------|----------|
| **Free** | $0/mo | Individual PM | 3 discovery cycles/bulan, basic AI guide |
| **Pro** | $29/mo per user | Startup teams | Unlimited cycles, advanced AI, integrations |
| **Team** | $49/mo per user | Mid-size teams | Team workspace, shared insights, dashboard |
| **Enterprise** | Custom pricing | Enterprise orgs | SSO, audit trail, custom AI, dedicated support |

**Revenue Projections (Year 1):**
- Free users: 5,000
- Pro conversions (5%): 250 users × $29/mo = $7,250/mo
- Team conversions (2%): 100 users × $49/mo = $4,900/mo
- **MRR Target End of Year 1: $12,150/mo (~$146K ARR)**

## Key Assumptions to Validate

### Must Be True (Critical)
1. **PMs akan membayar** untuk AI-powered discovery tools (bukan puas dengan ChatGPT gratis)
2. **AI bisa menghasilkan insight** yang cukup berkualitas untuk dipercaya oleh PMs
3. **Startup PMs** adalah beachhead segment yang tepat (burning pain + willingness to pay)
4. **Product discovery** dianggap cukup penting untuk diinvestasikan tools khusus

### Nice to Be True (Important)
5. Mid-size teams akan upgrade ke Team tier setelah individual adoption
6. Integration dengan Jira/Notion akan drive adoption secara signifikan
7. Discovery data flywheel akan menciptakan meaningful competitive moat

### Validation Experiments

| Experiment | Timeline | Success Metric |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| **Landing page + Waitlist** | Week 1-2 | 500+ signups |
| **Customer Interviews (20 PMs)** | Week 3-6 | 70%+ validate problem |
| **Concierge MVP (5 users)** | Week 7-14 | 3/5 complete full cycle |
| **Wizard of Oz Alpha** | Week 15-20 | 60%+ completion rate |
| **Private Beta (50 users)** | Week 21-30 | 20+ weekly active, 5+ paid |
